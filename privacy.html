Privacy Policy

Plain-English Summary

rewhatsapp was engineered from line one with an absolute principle: your chats never leave your browser. We do not operate a backend database, server-side parser, telemetry script, analytics cookie, or cloud processing pipeline. All parsing, timeline synthesis, and audio generation run locally on your hardware using native browser APIs.

1. Summary & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes the technical design, data practices, and legal framework governing the web application known as rewhatsapp ("we", "us", "the service"), accessible via this web portal and operated by qalvian.

This policy applies exclusively to your interactions with the rewhatsapp website. rewhatsapp is an independent, open-source personal memory replayer and is neither affiliated with, sponsored by, nor operated by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc.

2. Our Legal Position: Zero-Server Processing and Data Fiduciary Status

We do not collect, store, transmit, inspect, or retain your chat exports, message texts, or personal communications.

In plain legal terms: because we do not determine the purpose or means of processing personal data on our servers, and the user processes their own chat export on their own device for purely personal viewing, it is our understanding that we do not act as a "Data Fiduciary" or "Data Processor" under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA 2023) with respect to conversation contents.

Important Legal Note: This statement represents our architectural and legal understanding of client-side computing and is provided for transparency, not as a contractual warranty or legal guarantee.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

To eliminate any ambiguity, rewhatsapp does not collect, log, or transmit:

  • Chat Messages & Content: No text, voice transcripts, emoji reactions, or timestamps are ever uploaded.
  • Participant Contacts: Phone numbers, contact names, and profile avatars are parsed strictly in local RAM.
  • Media Attachments: Images, audio files, and video clips extracted from ZIP exports remain in local object URLs.
  • Identifiers & Telemetry: No device fingerprints, browser fingerprints, advertising IDs, or tracking beacons.
  • Application-Level IP Logs: The client application creates zero connection logs.
  • Cookies & Trackers: Zero third-party trackers, zero advertising scripts, and zero third-party cookie tags.

4. Local Storage Disclosure

rewhatsapp uses your browser's native localStorage API solely to store optional, non-sensitive user interface preferences across sessions. We do not use HTTP cookies.

  • rewhatsapp_speed: Stores your chosen replay multiplier (e.g., 2×, 5×).
  • rewhatsapp_muted: Stores your sound preference (true / false).
  • rewhatsapp_volume: Stores your preferred Web Audio volume level (0.0 to 1.0).
  • rewhatsapp_legal_consent_v1: Stores your acknowledgement of this policy and terms (true).

How to clear preferences: You can click the "Clear stored preferences" button in the Safe, Private & Offline section on the homepage, or manually clear your browser's site data in browser settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear Browsing Data → Cookies and Site Data).

5. Hosting & Server Logs

The static HTML, JavaScript, and CSS files comprising rewhatsapp are hosted on GitHub Pages.

When your browser requests our static assets, the underlying hosting provider's web servers may automatically record standard HTTP transmission metadata (including your IP address, request timestamp, HTTP referer, and browser user-agent) as an unavoidable technical part of serving web files over the Internet. This metadata is collected and processed directly by the hosting infrastructure provider under their own privacy practices and is outside our direct control.

6. Third-Party Personal Data Inside Chats

WhatsApp exports typically contain messages and identifiers belonging to third parties (such as friends, partners, family members, or group participants).

You are solely responsible for ensuring your use of exported chats complies with applicable law. We strongly encourage you to obtain the informed consent of other participants before replaying conversations, and to limit your use strictly to private, personal, non-commercial reminiscence.

7. Age & Eligibility

rewhatsapp is designed and intended for individuals aged 18 years or older. Minors may use the application only with the knowledge and supervision of a parent or lawful guardian. Because rewhatsapp does not collect personal data from anyone, we do not knowingly solicit or store information from children.

8. Security Architecture

We enforce privacy through hard technical safeguards enforced by your browser engine:

  • Content Security Policy (CSP): Our HTTP CSP header declares default-src 'none'; script-src 'self'; connect-src 'self';. The browser engine will forcibly abort any external outbound network transmission.
  • Sandboxed Web Workers: File parsing occurs inside isolated, origin-bound Worker threads that have no network capabilities.
  • Open Source Verification: The entire codebase is public on GitHub, allowing independent security researchers and users to audit and verify every line of code.

9. Rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA 2023) affords Indian citizens certain rights regarding their personal data, including the right to access summary of data, the right to correction and erasure, and the right of grievance redressal.

Because rewhatsapp holds no database, stores zero personal communications, and maintains no user accounts, we do not possess any data to access, correct, or erase. Once you close your browser tab, your chat content ceases to exist anywhere in the application.
