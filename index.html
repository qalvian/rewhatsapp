Export Guide

How to export your chat in 30 seconds.

WhatsApp lets you export any individual or group conversation directly from your phone into a lightweight, plain-text (.txt) file.

  1. Open chat in WhatsApp
    Open WhatsApp on your iPhone and tap into the specific chat you wish to relive.
  2. Tap contact or group name
    Tap the header bar at the top of your screen to open the contact information view.
  3. Select Export Chat
    Scroll to the bottom of the contact info page and tap Export Chat.
  4. Choose “Without Media”
    Select Without Media to generate a clean, lightweight .txt file containing your complete conversational history.
  5. Save to Files or Share
    Tap Save to Files or AirDrop the exported _chat.txt file to your computer.
  1. Open the conversation
    Open WhatsApp on your Android device and tap into the desired conversation.
  2. Tap the menu icon (⋮)
    Tap the three vertical dots menu icon in the top right corner of the chat screen.
  3. Tap More → Export chat
    Select More from the dropdown options, then choose Export chat.
  4. Select “Without media”
    Choose Without media to create a lightweight, portable .txt file containing your complete chat history.
  5. Save to Downloads
    Save the file directly to your local storage or send it to yourself via Google Drive or email.

What to do next

Once your .txt file is saved on your device, simply click Select chat file in the hero section above or drag and drop it onto this page. Your chat will organize into an interactive timeline in seconds with zero cloud uploads.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about exporting, replaying, and securing your WhatsApp chats.

Is my WhatsApp chat data uploaded to any server?
No. Absolutely nothing leaves your browser. All parsing, timeline sequencing, and audio synthesis happen directly in your device’s local runtime memory. You can even disconnect your Wi-Fi or turn on Airplane Mode after loading the page and replay your chats completely offline.
Does rewhatsapp support photos, voice notes, and videos?
rewhatsapp focuses exclusively on parsing and replaying text-based conversation exports (.txt files generated by selecting “Without Media”). Compressed archives (.zip) and multimedia attachments (photos, videos, and voice notes) are not currently supported.
Which WhatsApp export formats are supported?
rewhatsapp handles iOS bracketed formats [dd/mm/yy, hh:mm:ss], Android standard 12-hour and 24-hour formats dd/mm/yyyy, hh:mm - Name:, European dot-separated dates, and international AM/PM formats automatically.
Can I replay group chats as well as one-on-one chats?
Yes! When you upload a group chat, rewhatsapp detects all participants, assigns distinct color-coded sender tags, and allows you to pick your own persona during setup so outgoing messages appear properly on your side.
How does dynamic typing speed work?
The delay before each message appears is calculated directly by its character count (Base delay + 30ms per character). Outgoing messages simulate real typing directly in your composer bar, and playback speeds from −4× (slow reading pace) up to +4× can be adjusted at any moment.
Does this work offline?
Yes. rewhatsapp registers a progressive web app service worker so you can save the app to your home screen or desktop and replay exported chats even when you have zero internet connectivity.
