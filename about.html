About rewhatsapp

A time machine for the moments that mattered

We built rewhatsapp to prove two things: that digital conversations deserve more than a cold, flat scrollback—and that an exceptional modern web experience can be built with uncompromising, 100% private client-side engineering.

1. Why it was built

Some conversations are too good to stay buried in a chat list.

Friendships that survived cross-country moves, the giddy nervousness of a first crush, late-night inside jokes that kept you laughing through college exams, and the long-distance months where a simple "Good morning" message was the anchor of your day: they all live in a scrolling wall of text that we almost never revisit.

Reading a raw export in a text editor or scrolling through months of messages is cold. It loses the suspense of the three bouncing dots. It loses the hesitation before a confession. It loses the late-night timing and the sudden burst of rapid-fire replies.

rewhatsapp was built to give those moments a second life: replaying the conversation the exact way it happened, with the pauses, the cadence, and the gentle audio pops, so you can truly feel it again instead of just reading it.

2. Who it is for

We designed this replayer with specific human connections in mind:

  • Couples & Partners: To rewind to the night it started, the early hesitations, the first inside jokes, and the anniversaries that followed.
  • Lifelong Friends: To look back on college years, road trips, group banter, and memories with friends you might not see every day anymore.
  • Families Across Distances: To preserve the everyday warmth of parents, siblings, and grandparents whose words you cherish.
  • Anyone Remembering Someone Dear: To revisit conversations with people who brought laughter and joy into your life.

3. What it will never do

We live in an era where software companies routinely harvest intimate user data for ad targeting, training models, or venture capital metrics. We reject that model outright.

  • It will never upload your chats. Everything stays inside your local browser memory.
  • It will never show ads. No banners, no popups, no sponsored placements.
  • It will never track you. No Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, no telemetry beacons.
  • It will never require an account. No logins, no passwords, no email signups.
  • It will never charge you. It is free, open source, and built under the PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 license.

4. The craft & engineering philosophy

rewhatsapp was engineered with zero runtime dependencies. There is no React, no heavy framework, no npm bundle bloat, and no remote asset loading.

The sound system generates audio procedurally on your device using the Web Audio API with a synthesized convolution reverb and lowpass filter. The parsing engine runs in streaming Web Workers. The animations use native Web Animations API and CSS transforms. The entire application loads in milliseconds and operates flawlessly offline as an installable PWA.

5. A gentle note on using it respectfully

Conversations are shared intimate spaces. When you use rewhatsapp, remember that you are holding not only your own words, but someone else’s thoughts, feelings, and vulnerability.

Please use this tool with grace, kindness, and respect. Only replay conversations you were a part of, and honor the trust of the person who shared those smiles with you.
